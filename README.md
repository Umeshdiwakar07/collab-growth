# Collab Growth 🚀

**Collab Growth** is a collaborative marketing platform that connects small businesses with content creators to promote products and services.

## 🌟 About the Project

Small businesses often struggle to reach a wider audience due to limited marketing budgets. At the same time, content creators look for opportunities to collaborate with brands.

Collab Growth bridges this gap by providing a common platform where businesses and creators can connect, collaborate, and grow together.

## 🎯 Key Features

- Business registration and profiles
- Content creator registration and profiles
- Discover and connect with creators and businesses
- Collaboration opportunities
- Product and brand promotion
- Campaign management
- A common platform for business growth

## 👥 Target Users

- Small business owners
- Influencers and content creators
- Emerging brands and startups

## 💡 Project Vision

To make digital marketing more accessible by creating meaningful partnerships between small businesses and content creators.
